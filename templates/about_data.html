Where our data comes from.

Career Compass is built on a simple principle: recommendations should be grounded in real, citable data — not invented by an AI model. Here's exactly what we use, and where it falls short.

school

O*NET (U.S. Department of Labor)

We use O*NET's occupational database to power our skills and career-path matching. It provides a detailed, standardized taxonomy of occupations, required skills, and job characteristics. This part of the taxonomy is largely universal, not tied to the US job market specifically.

public

World Bank Open Data

Used for macro-level labor market trends specific to Pakistan — things like employment structure and workforce trends at the national level.

bar_chart

ILOSTAT (International Labour Organization)

Used for Pakistan-specific wage and labor market statistics, based on Pakistan's own Labour Force Survey and ILO's standardized international statistics.

description

Pakistan Bureau of Statistics

Key figures from PBS labour force survey reports are manually reviewed and cited, since PBS does not currently offer a public API for this data.

info

A limitation we want to be upfront about

Granular, real-time Pakistani job market data (e.g. live salary postings by company) is not currently available through any public API. Where our data is global or US-sourced rather than Pakistan-specific, we say so directly in your report, rather than presenting it as more precise than it actually is.
